# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Yard staff scanning vehicles for logistics tracking, admins managing yard inventory, and delivery in-charge checking vehicles out. They are typically working in a fast-paced physical environment, possibly outdoors or in a warehouse, using mobile devices to scan QR codes and track vehicle status.

## Product Purpose
To provide a fast, reliable, and real-time inventory tracking system for the Nippon Toyota yard. It ensures vehicles are accurately logged in and out, flags discrepancies or damage, and provides clear visibility into the current stock across multiple yard locations. Success means zero bottlenecks in the scanning workflow and accurate tracking.

## Brand Personality
Fast, reliable, utilitarian, industrial.

## Anti-references
- Bloated SaaS dashboards with too much whitespace and unnecessary animations
- Consumer-style apps with playful or overly colorful themes
- Heavy interfaces that require slow loading times or complex navigation

## Design Principles
- **Utilitarian efficiency:** The UI must be fast, readable in sunlight, and require minimal taps.
- **High contrast:** Important actions and status indicators must be immediately obvious.
- **Data density where appropriate:** Staff need to see information quickly without excessive scrolling.
- **Resilient to poor connectivity:** The app must function reliably even if the network is spotty.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High contrast text for readability in various lighting conditions
- Large touch targets for users who might be wearing gloves or moving quickly
- Clear error states and feedback mechanisms
